# Smoky Mountains Fall Trip Planner

An interactive day-by-day planner for a Great Smoky Mountains trip in October 2026. It covers a
two-stage drive with a Friday hotel stop, a Gatlinburg cabin, and a catalog of about 180 hikes,
scenic drives, attractions, coffee stops and restaurants, with 559 real, openly licensed photos.

**Open the planner:** https://zmcdonald2006-bit.github.io/smoky-trip-planner/

## How it works

- Everything runs in your browser. There's no account and no server-side storage, and nothing is ever booked.
- Your plan autosaves **in the browser you're using**. Use *Print & share → Export plan* to back it
  up or move it to another device.
- Private details you type (cabin address, entry notes, confirmation numbers) and photos you add
  stay only in that browser. They're never part of this website.
- Hours, prices and dates come from research done October 4–5, 2026. Recheck before you go.
  Drive and travel times are planning estimates, not live traffic.
