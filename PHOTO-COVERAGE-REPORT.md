# Photo coverage report

Built 2026-10-08. **559 photos** across **108 places**, all packaged locally in `assets/photos/` (a 960px view plus a 400px thumbnail each). Sources: 322 from Flickr, 237 from Wikimedia Commons.

## How photos were chosen

- Searched Wikimedia Commons (categories and full-text), Flickr Creative Commons (via Openverse) and NPS park pages for each place by name.
- Kept only photos with an explicit reuse basis: CC BY / BY-SA / BY-NC / BY-ND variants, CC0, Public Domain Mark, or U.S. government (NPS/HAER) public domain. Operator, hotel, Airbnb and tourism-bureau galleries are copyrighted, so they're **linked, not packaged**.
- Every photo was viewed and matched to the exact place: title, tags, description and the source page's location. Rejected: other branches or same-named places elsewhere (Myrtle Beach, Sedona, Niagara Falls, Big Bend…), AI-generated images, heavily filtered or recolored shots, selfie-centred photos, alcohol close-ups, winter/holiday shots presented as typical, and anything naming the two excluded attractions.
- No crops of one photo were used to pad a gallery. Images are resized only (no edits).
- Capture dates come from the photo's own metadata or the photographer's caption (553 of 559 dated; the rest say “capture date unknown”). 182 are dated October/November. Older, holiday, summer, winter and special-event photos say so in the caption or tags.
